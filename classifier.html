ImageClassifier: poetic subtitle overlay

ImageClassifier Demo

怎么用 How to use: 把不同物体放到摄像头前，比如杯子、手机、书、本子。页面下方会持续显示 AI 对当前画面的分类标签和置信度。 Show different objects to the camera, such as a cup, phone, book, or notebook. The page will keep displaying the AI's current label and confidence score.

建议修改 Try changing first: 先改字幕的语言和风格。可以直接显示英文标签，也可以把它改成中文诗句、讽刺性文字或自己的概念文本。 Start by changing the subtitle language and visual tone. You can keep the raw English label or rewrite it as Chinese poetry, satire, or your own conceptual text.

原理 Principle: 这个 demo 用 ml5.imageClassifier() 调用预训练分类模型。模型会定期读取摄像头画面，输出“它认为看到了什么”，程序再把这个结果变成字幕式文本叠加到视频上。 This demo uses ml5.imageClassifier() with a pretrained image model. The model periodically reads the camera image, predicts what it sees, and the program turns that result into subtitle-like text on the video.
