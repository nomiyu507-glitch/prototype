BodyPose: webcam + skeleton + particles

BodyPose Demo

怎么用 How to use: 打开页面并允许摄像头权限，站到画面前活动身体。页面会在你身上画出骨架线、关键点圆点，并从手腕和鼻子位置发射粒子。 Open the page, allow camera access, and move your body in front of the screen. The sketch draws a body skeleton, keypoint dots, and particles coming from your wrists and nose.

建议修改 Try changing first: 先改 TARGET_KEYPOINTS，决定哪些身体部位触发效果；再改粒子的颜色、数量和大小，就能快速做出自己的版本。 Start with TARGET_KEYPOINTS to choose which body parts trigger the effect, then adjust particle color, amount, and size.

原理 Principle: 这个 demo 用 ml5.bodyPose() 识别人体关键点的位置，比如鼻子、手腕、肩膀和膝盖。程序先把关键点连成骨架，再读取选中的关键点坐标，把它们当成粒子的发射位置。 This demo uses ml5.bodyPose() to detect body keypoints such as the nose, wrists, shoulders, and knees. The program connects them into a skeleton and uses selected keypoint positions as particle emitters.
